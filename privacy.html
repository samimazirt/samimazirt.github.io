ORBIT

Privacy Policy · Last updated: November 16, 2026

Summary

Orbit has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or contacts. Your best score is stored only on your device. The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not run our own servers and do not receive any of your data.
  • Your best score and your “No Ads” purchase status are stored locally on your device.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center, under Apple’s privacy policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Orbit uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads. Google may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers (IDFA, only if you allow tracking, and IDFV);
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • Ad interactions and app usage data (e.g. ads shown, taps, app launches);
  • Diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

This data is used to show, measure and improve ads, and to prevent fraud. If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Orbit asks for your consent through Google’s consent form; you can change your choices with the PRIVACY button on the home screen.

Learn more: How Google uses data from partner apps and Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Orbit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights

Since we do not hold any of your personal data, requests about advertising data should be made to Google. For any question, contact us at CONTACT_EMAIL.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Politique de confidentialité (FR)

Orbit n’a pas de compte et ne demande ni nom, ni e-mail, ni contacts. Ton meilleur score reste sur ton appareil. Les achats sont traités par Apple, et les classements par Game Center.

Le jeu affiche des publicités Google AdMob. Google peut collecter : identifiants de l’appareil (IDFA uniquement si tu autorises le suivi, et IDFV), localisation approximative déduite de l’adresse IP, interactions avec les annonces et l’app, données de diagnostic. Ces données servent à afficher et mesurer les publicités et à lutter contre la fraude.

Tu peux refuser le suivi dans Réglages › Confidentialité et sécurité › Suivi. Dans l’Union européenne, Orbit te demande ton consentement via le formulaire de Google ; tu peux modifier tes choix avec le bouton PRIVACY de l’écran d’accueil.
