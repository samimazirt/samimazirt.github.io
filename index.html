FAQ

How do I remove ads?
On the home screen, tap NO ADS. Already bought it on another device? Tap RESTORE.

What does “Continue” do?
After a crash, you can watch a short video to keep your score and level. Once per game.

How do I change my privacy choices?
Tap PRIVACY on the home screen (shown where required), or go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
